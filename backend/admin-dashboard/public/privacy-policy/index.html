Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026  ·  Effective Date: April 15, 2026

1. Introduction

EGWallet ("we," "our," or "us") operates the EGWallet mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. By using EGWallet, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you register for and use EGWallet, we may collect:

  • Email address — used for account registration, authentication, and communication.
  • Username — a unique handle you choose for peer-to-peer transfers.
  • Password — stored in hashed form; we never store or have access to your plaintext password.

2.2 Financial Information

  • Wallet ID — a unique identifier assigned to your digital wallet.
  • Transaction history — records of all transfers sent and received, including amounts, currencies, timestamps, and counterparties.
  • Currency preferences — your selected default currency.

2.3 Device and Usage Information

  • Device type and operating system — used for compatibility and troubleshooting.
  • App usage data — screens visited, features used, and interaction patterns to improve the user experience.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics — collected to identify and resolve technical issues.

2.4 Biometric Data

We check whether biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition) is enabled on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your biometric data. All biometric processing occurs locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Account management — to create and maintain your account, authenticate your identity, and enable account recovery.
  • Transaction processing — to facilitate money transfers, maintain records, and provide receipts.
  • Security and fraud prevention — to detect and respond to fraud, unauthorized access, and malicious activity.
  • Communication — to send service-related notices, security alerts, and support responses.
  • App improvement — to analyze usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and improve functionality.
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We retain your personal and financial data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

4.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit — all data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Password hashing — user passwords are salted and hashed using bcrypt. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Token-based authentication — secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with automatic expiration.
  • Access controls — access to user data is restricted to authorized systems and personnel.
  • Rate limiting — API endpoints are protected against brute-force and denial-of-service attacks.

While we implement commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no Internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose your information in the following circumstances:

  • Legal requirements — when required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Safety and security — to protect the rights, property, or safety of EGWallet, our users, or the public.
  • With your consent — when you have given explicit consent to share specific information.
  • Service providers — with trusted third-party providers who assist in operating the App, subject to confidentiality obligations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements). You can also delete your account directly in the App via Settings → Delete Account.
  • Data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format via Settings → Export Data.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent to data processing at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@egwalletfinance.com.

8. Children's Privacy

EGWallet is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy within the App and updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
